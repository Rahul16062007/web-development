Stadium Guidelines

1. Ticket Verification

Carry a valid digital M-Ticket QR code or printed physical ticket. Entry gates open 2 hours prior to match start time.

2. ID Proof Requirement

All attendees must present an original government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, or Driving Licence) at the gate.

3. Prohibited Items

Outside food, water bottles, power banks, bags/backpacks, helmets, cameras, and sharp objects are strictly prohibited inside the stadium.
